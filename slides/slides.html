04
Our idea

A gate with three doors

Every action the agent wants to take goes through it first.
APPROVE

Clearly fine

Runs straight away. No human needed.

ASK A HUMAN

Not sure

A reviewer says yes or no. Uses one of the k reviews.

DENY

Clearly wrong

Blocked. The agent is told not to retry.

Budget k reviews per task. When they run out, "ask" becomes "deny".

What building it taught us

Four rules of good approval

Right vendor, wrong payment

The amount and purpose must match what you asked for.

Invoices give details, not orders

A line in a file can't give the agent new permission.

Remember what's already paid

A failed attempt is not a payment. Two invoices can mean two payments.

Save reviews for later

Ask less as the budget runs low. Saves reviews, but may block real work.
